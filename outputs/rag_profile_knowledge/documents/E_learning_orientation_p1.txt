  - expected_highest_edu_e
  - past_edu_usefulness
  - plans_further_edu

Theoretical basis and purpose: Perceived usefulness of past education combined with orientation toward further learning and expected highest education level.

Clustering method: Gower distance over original mixed-type responses (no invented 0-100
scores), consensus of repeated K-medoids and spectral clustering across 100 matched
80% subsamples (200 base partitions), final clusters from K-medoids on (1 - co-association).

Validation diagnostics for this retained model: Gower silhouette 0.567; bootstrap/subsample ARI 0.953; source-method ARIs 0.882 and 1.000; permutation p=0.040; 10 of 10 mandatory validation checks passed.
Final decision status: Retained cautiously; strongly dependent on past education usefulness.
Model-level caution (from the frozen decision register): Branch E has only 34 respondents and the retained solution depends strongly on perceived past-education usefulness.

Additional definitional context: Removing past_edu_usefulness from the model changed cluster membership almost completely (ARI 0.039), meaning this single item effectively drives the partition. The correct narrow label is 'education-usefulness and further-learning orientation' — it must NOT be presented as a validated human-capital, readiness or ability classification. Branch E is the smallest and most sample-constrained branch (34 of 362 strict-cohort respondents); replication with more unemployed respondents is particularly important before any operational use.

Interpretation of this specific profile (verbatim from the frozen report's per-model
profile table):
  83.3% rated past education very useful; 94.4% planned further education; 66.7% expected postgraduate education.

Reminder: this profile exists only WITHIN this one retained dimension. A respondent's
membership here says nothing about their membership in any other branch's or dimension's
profile system (see PROHIBITED_INFERENCES).
