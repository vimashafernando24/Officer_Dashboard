## Evidence and mapping rules

1. Use official government, qualification-authority, awarding-body or verified public-provider sources. A title, search snippet or registration listing alone is not curriculum evidence.
2. Preserve exact source URLs, document version/date, retrieval date, relevant published references and PDF SHA-256 hashes. Exclude sources whose substantive content cannot be inspected.
3. Keep `programme`, `national_curriculum`, `occupational_standard` and `national_qualification` distinct. A standard or qualification is not a confirmed delivery; `delivering_provider_verified` may be true only for a verified provider programme.
4. Reuse an existing record only where its stored modules/outcomes substantively fit the target occupation. An incidental module is not enough to make a programme an occupation comparator.
5. Create a competency matrix only from usable governed NYSC headings. Preserve exact governed text and approved display corrections; never infer additional NYSC competencies from a title.
6. `DOCUMENTED` requires an exact stored citation. `FULL` means every declared component is explicit; `PARTIAL` means only part or a broader/lower-level activity is explicit. `NOT_EXPLICIT` means not found in the assessed source scope, not necessarily absent from delivery. `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` means the captured source cannot support a reliable decision.
7. `FULL_COMPARISON` requires linked verified comparators and a completed NYSC mapping. With verified comparators but no defensible governed headings, use `COMPARATORS_ONLY`. With no verified linked records, retain `RESEARCH_PENDING`.
8. An Area to Investigate is generated only when the NYSC cell is `NOT_EXPLICIT` and at least two comparator records from at least two distinct countries document the competency with `FULL` coverage. `PARTIAL` and `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` never satisfy the threshold.
9. Do not manually edit coverage totals or frontend imports. Update evidence, mappings and course links, then run `src/rag_prototype/build_coverage_register.py` to regenerate `coverage_register.json`, `coverageIndex.json` and `comparisonDataLoaders.ts`.
10. New research stays `source_inspected_pending_human_review` until independent human review. The review state remains in data even though the orange comparator-card badge is intentionally hidden.
